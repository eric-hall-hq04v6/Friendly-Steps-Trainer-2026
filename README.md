## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Friendly Steps**. This trainer provides optional assistance for Friendly Steps' physics-driven walking and cooperative traversal. It can improve balance, reduce the impact of falls, stabilize movement, and make difficult routes easier to explore without adding combat or inventory systems that are not part of the game.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed way to practice physics-based walking, explore difficult routes, complete optional objectives, or enjoy cooperative sessions without repeated falls and movement failures.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Friendly Steps requires independent control of each leg, constant balance management, and careful movement across uneven surfaces. Activate Balance Assist or Movement Stabilizer before attempting steep hills, stairs, bridges, playgrounds, construction areas, or narrow shortcuts. Use the assistance sparingly when practicing so you can still learn the game's normal movement timing.

### Tools & Inventory

Friendly Steps does not use a conventional inventory system. The trainer does not create items, alter equipment, or provide inventory management functions. Exploration-related assistance is intended for reaching objects, side paths, and optional objectives placed throughout the world.

### World & Environment

The game focuses on finding a way home through varied urban environments while dealing with physics-based hazards. Use Fall Protection when exploring elevated routes or testing risky shortcuts. In speedrun-style play, movement assistance can help with route practice, but normal game rules should be restored before recording official times.

### Combat & Weapons

Friendly Steps has no traditional combat system. Encounters and hazards are handled through movement, physics, environmental navigation, emotes, and cooperative coordination rather than weapons or character attacks.
